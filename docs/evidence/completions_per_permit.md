## Reading

- **Like for like.** The baseline counts permits in permit-issuing places, so the conversion uses completions in the same places. All-area completions add homes built where no permit is needed (about 2.5% of all homes, nearly all single-family, per Census), which is why the research session's one-unit ratio was above 1.
- **Two to four units.** Census publishes 2-4 unit completions, but they are not a completion rate: permit offices often file townhouses as multifamily, and the Survey of Construction reclassifies them as one-unit structures (https://www.census.gov/construction/pdf/nrc_relationships.pdf, p. 1). The 2-4 entry therefore uses the all-structures ratio, which reclassification cannot move.
- **Five or more units.** About a fifth of apartment units permitted are never completed or are re-permitted; the two completion series agree because almost no apartments are built outside permit-issuing places.
- **Low end.** 2010-2024 includes the slow recovery, when more permits lapsed and apartment pipelines lengthened; the window's last years are still under construction, which also lowers it.
